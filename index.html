Gameplay

Player 1

Use the A and D keys to move left and right, W to jump, and Space to attack.
Player 1 has a longer blade with a slower swing, so time your swings wisely!

Player 2

Use the ← and → arrow keys to move left and right, ↑ to jump, and ↓ to attack.
Player 2 has a shorter blade that swings faster, so don't be afraid to rush in!
